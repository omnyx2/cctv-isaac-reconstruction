How does it work?

Eyes, brain, hands

👀 Eyes

YOLO draws boxes around cars and people.

ByteTrack keeps car #1 as car #1 in every frame.

🧠 Brain

A lane is 3.5 m wide: four points on the lane lines turn pixels into meters.

A Kalman filter removes the jitter.

🎮 Hands

A bicycle model lets cars move only like real cars.

Isaac Sim places them in the 3D world.

Why not just teleport the car to the AI's guess? The AI is a little wrong sometimes. Teleporting would make cars slide sideways or spin on the spot.

Folders: perception (eyes), world (brain), simulation (hands), config (settings per video), main.py starts everything. Look for calibration_points in config/detrac_MVI_20011.yaml to see the pixel-to-meter points.
