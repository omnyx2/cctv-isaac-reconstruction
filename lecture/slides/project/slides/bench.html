32 CCTV frames, 18 crash photos, 8 look-alikes · no model raised a false alarm · lecture/vlm_bench/results/REPORT.md

Before class we asked every vision model we had the same five tasks about 58 pictures: 32 CCTV frames with no accident, 18 real crash photos, and 8 tricky photos that look like accidents but are not. Words per second is really tokens per second. Accident right = the yes/no accident answer in the scene JSON. Crashes found = how many of the 18 crash photos the model called an accident. None of the models called a normal picture an accident. The tiny gemma4 is fast but missed most crashes.
