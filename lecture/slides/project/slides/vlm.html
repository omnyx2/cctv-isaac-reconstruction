A VLM is a vision-language model: it looks at the whole picture and answers in words. The script starts the Ollama server (runs AI models on our own computer, no internet needed after download), downloads the model once, and asks about 3 frames of MVI_20011. Answers go to data/results/vlm/lecture_test.jsonl. Measured on 2026-10-11. 30B-A3B means 30 billion numbers in total but only 3 billion work on each word, so it answers like a small model. 4-bit means each number is squeezed into 4 bits.
